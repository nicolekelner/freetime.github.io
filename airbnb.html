My approach

Build for trust

I design products to help the mental health and wellbeing of my users. My passion this year has been building apps that help people redefine their relationship with technology by reducing screen time, helping them be more present and mindful in their lives.

What brings me here

Airbnb is hiring a Product Manager to own discounts and promotions: design the offer, decide who sees it and when, and prove it moved something. Pricing is the part of my own products I have spent this year inside, and it is the part I find most interesting, because a price is the clearest question you can ask a user and the answer is never the one you assumed.
