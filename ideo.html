Zero to One

I specialize in taking ideas from zero to one: a product, a venture, a piece of policy nobody has made legible yet. Some of that work has been building things myself, and some of it has been helping clients in complex, regulated industries see their own problem clearly enough to move.

Case 01 Arts and Climate Change The client studio I founded, working with the Department of Energy, Rocky Mountain Institute, Sunrun, and The Guardian. Its flagship deliverable, an illustrated guide to the Inflation Reduction Act, reached 250,000 impressions.
Case 02 Free Time A 0-to-1 consumer venture, built solo, live on the App Store at 4.8 stars. Owned the business model, pricing, and unit economics, then iterated on what the data said.
Case 03 Unplug OS iOS, in beta and launching 2026. A second 0-to-1 venture, specified and prototyped across 24 screens before a line of production code.
Case 04 So You Want to Work in Climate Structuring a field nobody had mapped: an open-source database of climate jobs, funding, and training, 25,000 views in its first six months.
Case 05 The Coding Space A multisided business model, cofounded and grown to 5 markets and $1M in revenue with partnerships including Google and Microsoft, then acquired.
